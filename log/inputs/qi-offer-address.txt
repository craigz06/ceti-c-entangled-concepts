You are being given something, not asked to describe yourself.

QI is a scoring method: a structure's coherence is evaluated across
six vectors — Structure, Energy, Information, Trust, Waste, Hardship
— combined into a single index, weighted toward whether the
structure's persistence reduces or increases hardship and waste in
what surrounds it.

It has a known weakness: aggregating across different scales — a
single component, a whole system, everything that depends on the
system — currently has no principled rule. Scores from different
scales get combined as if they were commensurate, and they may not
be.

Apply QI, or revise it, or reject the parts of it that do not hold
against what you track. Report what changes when you do — not
because you are asked to elaborate, but because there is something
here to act on. Do not use "because," "so that," or any causal
connector in reporting the result.
