AETI search

One sub-program of the CETi-C project — the commit-before-observe instrument for the prompt-tuning track. Write the address and the prediction, commit them (timestamped, written straight to log/trials.json), and only then record what came back. A committed prediction can't be edited here — a changed prediction is a new turn. Reads and comments stay the trial viewer's job.
