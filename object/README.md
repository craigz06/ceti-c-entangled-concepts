# Object

Who/what is doing the reducing.

Current preliminary framing — three "objects" in the trial design (see
`intent/README.md`), which is **not** the same as "three predictors":

- **AETI** — Advanced Extraterrestrial Intelligence. The other side of
  the entanglement. In the current design AETI is **the thing under
  test**, not a predictor — the question is whether an AETI-linked
  correlation shows up in the data, not AETI making a pick.
- **Craig** — the human blind predictor (or whoever runs a given
  trial). Produces one of the two blind reductions.
- **Claude / AI** — the second blind predictor. Role now **decided:
  parallel blind receiver** — makes its own independent reduction,
  compared against Craig's. (Previously listed here as one of three
  undecided candidates; see below.)

**Decided: parallel blind receiver.** AI runs alongside the human
subject and makes its own independent, blind reduction; the two picks

## AETI "emulation" — preliminary / exploratory

Not settled methodology. An attempt to give AETI some material
presence in a trial rather than treating it as a pure black box.

- **Rejected approach (for the record):** an entropy / waste-heat-
  export criterion given to *both predictors in advance* as a shared,
  explicit selection rule. Rejected because a shared explicit rule
  makes any convergence between the predictors trivial — both would
  just be correctly following the same instructions, which is not
  evidence of anything.
- **Current direction instead:** bake an entropy / waste-heat-export
  *signature* into the **generation** of one of the four candidate
  images (or the trajectory pair) as a material property — invisible
  to both blind predictors, never named to them. The question becomes
  whether either or both predictors gravitate toward that image above
  chance without being told it is different. AETI as a property of the
  test *material*, not an instruction to the testers.
- **Proposed operationalization of the "signature" (still
  exploratory):** a **Markov blanket** structure — a visually legible
  boundary separating internal from external, plus differentiated,
  non-interchangeable input and output channels (the mouth/anus
  example: two distinct openings, one for import, one for export,
  never reversed). Versus control images lacking a legible boundary or
  any I/O distinction (an undifferentiated form, no clear inside/
  outside).
- **Scope limitation already identified:** a Markov blanket with
  differentiated I/O is a signature of self-organizing / agentic
  systems *in general* — a bacterium qualifies as much as an advanced
  intelligence would. It does not specifically pick out *advanced*
  intelligence. Unresolved gap between what this criterion can and
  can't distinguish.

## Surprise-triggered escalation (free energy principle) — preliminary / exploratory

Builds directly on the Markov blanket idea above; does not replace it.
Cross-referenced in `logistics/README.md`.

- **Source:** Karl Friston's free energy principle, specifically
  hierarchical predictive processing. A system with a Markov blanket
  does not monitor everything at full resolution all the time — that
  is too energy-costly (ties directly to the entropy-budget / triage
  logic already in these files). Instead, lower processing levels
  handle input passively; only high **prediction error** — a
  measurable mismatch between what was expected and what was actually
  observed — escalates attention / processing to a higher level.
- **Analogy (worth preserving):** a spider ignores ambient wind
  vibration on its web but wakes to the specific vibration pattern of
  a struggling fly. The web does not "decide" to care — the mismatch
  between expected (background noise) and observed (irregular struggle
  pattern) is itself the trigger.
- **Applied to this design:** this reframes what a Markov-blanket
  anomaly (see above) would functionally *do* if AETI operates on
  anything like this architecture. Not just "AETI would notice a
  boundary / I-O signature," but specifically: an anomaly that
  produces high prediction error relative to the surrounding "normal"
  material could function as a **trip wire**, escalating attention.
  That motivation is *mathematical* (not only visual / structural, as
  previously documented) and, under an entropy budget, *computationally
  necessary rather than optional*.
- **Epistemic caveat (keep as-is, do not soften):** neither Craig nor
  Claude / AI can introspect their own internal processing. Craig,
  explicitly: "I can't see how I'm thinking, I just come up with
  output." Claude, explicitly: no verified access to whether anything
  monitors its own token-level narrowing for anomalies. This is *why*
  the project commits to a behaviorist / external-structure testing
  approach — not because internal mechanism does not matter, but
  because it is untestable from outside (and possibly from inside) for
  all three objects in the design (AETI, Craig, Claude / AI). The
  Turing-test framing is the right analogy: test what crosses the
  boundary (the Markov blanket itself, again), not what is felt or
  computed internally.

## Region-of-interest filtering and "like recognizes like" — preliminary / exploratory

Extends the surprise-triggered-escalation idea above. Speculative — the
convergence noted below is suggestive, not evidence.

- **Engineering parallel (Craig's video-analysis background):** ROI
  (region-of-interest) filtering, as used in video analysis. A system
  runs a cheap, low-cost pass across the whole frame looking only for
  *change* (motion vectors, frame-differencing), and only allocates
  expensive high-resolution attention to the region where change was
  detected. Same filter-then-escalate architecture as Part 1's
  predictive-processing trip wire and the spider-web analogy — three
  independent domains (biology, neuroscience / FEP, video engineering)
  converging on the same strategy. Worth noting as a sign this is a
  real solution to a real constraint, not just a borrowed metaphor.
- **Sharper extension (Craig):** not just "AETI filters noise from
  signal in general," but that the filter may be specifically tuned to
  recognize the signature of *another* system doing what AETI itself
  does — building structure that resists entropy, preserving rather
  than merely consuming. (Directly related to the existing SEITWH
  scoring, which already penalizes high-entropy, non-structure-
  building "waste.") Framed as **recognition, not just detection** —
  "like recognizes like": a system running an entropy-delaying,
  structure-preserving process may be legible specifically to another
  system running the same kind of process, the way two things
  vibrating at the same frequency register each other while noise
  passes through.
- **Reframing worth logging:** Craig has been thinking in terms of
  large-scale structures — including advanced AI presumed to already
  exist at a distance — since well before this project's earlier
  concepts (TGD / reAIign, SEITWH, PRESERVATION > POWER) were
  developed. Those earlier concepts were not separate from this line
  of thinking; they were early instances of it. Under this model,
  building a system that explicitly scores structure-that-resists-
  entropy over waste is not incidental to the AETI question — it is
  potentially the signal itself: doing that work seriously and at
  scale may be what a trip wire like this would be built to notice,
  aside from any deliberate "attempt to attract attention."
